SPHEREx Moving Sky

NASA's SPHEREx telescope photographs the entire sky every six months. Things that move between those photos are asteroids, comets, and maybe something new. This tool finds them automatically, then checks itself against every known asteroid in JPL's catalogue.

Blink the sky

Each frame is a separate SPHEREx exposure, resampled onto the same patch of sky. Stars stay still; moving dots are solar-system objects.

Detected tracks

Does it work? Recovery of known asteroids

For every asteroid JPL predicts inside an exposure, we measure the signal at the predicted pixel, then confirm the spot is empty hours later.
